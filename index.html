Your Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know before your first page.

What is Today App? +

Today is a privacy-first, 100% offline journal. One honest page per day — locks at midnight. No account, no cloud, no clutter. It's built for people who want to think clearly, not manage another productivity system.

How does pricing work? +

7-day free trial — no card needed. After that, a one-time purchase. No subscription. No recurring billing. No account required. You pay once, you own it forever.

Is Today for me if I'm not a "journaling person"? +

Especially yes. Today isn't a diary — it's a daily thinking tool. You don't need eloquent entries. Write what's on your mind, what you want from the day, what you figured out. Three sentences is enough. There's no wrong way.

Does Today sync to the cloud? +

No. Not at all. Today is architected to be offline-first — your journal lives entirely on your device. No sync, no server, no analytics on your writing. Your thoughts are yours, full stop.

What does immutable history mean? +

After 24 hours, yesterday's entry becomes read-only — forever. You can still read it, but not edit it. This keeps your past authentic and forces forward momentum instead of endless revision.

What is Memory Rail, exactly? +

Memory Rail is Today's signature feature. As you write, it quietly surfaces relevant entries from your past — no searching required. It runs entirely on-device, so nothing is ever sent anywhere. Your second brain, not a cloud AI.
